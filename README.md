## 👋 Hi, I'm Ratan

I'm a B.Tech student at **IIITDM Jabalpur** passionate about building AI products that bridge research and real-world impact.

My interests span:

- 🤖 Generative AI & LLM Applications
- 🏥 Healthcare Intelligence Systems
- 📚 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- ⚙️ Full-Stack Product Development
- 🧠 Machine Learning & System Design

I enjoy transforming complex workflows into practical products using **Generative AI**, **Machine Learning**, and **Full-Stack Engineering**.

From modernizing legacy COBOL systems to developing healthcare intelligence platforms and RAG-powered applications, I love building systems that people can actually use.
